
# 📊 Adidas Sales Dashboard (Power BI)

## 📌 Overview
Developed a multi-page interactive Power BI dashboard to analyze Adidas sales performance across regions, product categories, and sales channels. The dashboard provides a comprehensive view of revenue, profitability, and operational efficiency, enabling data-driven business insights.

---

## 🎯 Objectives
- Monitor overall sales performance and profitability  
- Analyze regional and retailer-wise contributions  
- Identify top-performing product categories  
- Evaluate sales channels and operational efficiency  

---

## 🧭 Dashboard Structure

### 🏠 Landing Page
- Introductory overview of the dashboard  
- Navigation buttons for seamless transition between pages  
- Designed for intuitive user experience  

---

### 📈 Executive Sales Summary
- KPI Cards:
  - Total Revenue: **39.28M**
  - Total Operating Profit: **13.53M**
  - Avg Operating Margin: **40.9%**
  - Total Units Sold: **104K**
- Monthly Revenue & Margin Trends  
- Sales distribution by method (Online, In-store, Outlet)  
- Region-wise revenue and profit analysis  
- Geographic sales distribution (map visualization)  
- Product category performance trends  

---

### 🏬 Retailer & Product Performance
- State-wise retailer distribution (map)  
- Retailer performance comparison (Revenue, Profit, Margin)  
- Profit efficiency analysis (Units Sold vs Profit)  
- Category contribution mix across retailers  
- Top product category: **Street Footwear**  
- Top retailer by revenue: **Sports Direct**  

---

## 🚀 Key Features
- 🔄 Fully interactive dashboard with slicers and filters  
- 📊 Multi-page navigation for structured analysis  
- 🌍 Geographic visualization for regional insights  
- 📈 Combination of bar, line, pie, and scatter charts

## 📈 Insights Generated
- Southeast region generates the highest revenue contribution  
- Online sales channel dominates total unit sales (~67%)  
- Street Footwear emerges as the top-performing product category  
- Sports Direct leads among retailers in terms of revenue  
- Seasonal trends indicate higher sales during mid-year and year-end
